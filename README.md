## Working with the Site

Apex Forge can serve as the foundation for a fitness promotion page, coaching business site, or service offer landing page.

A standard customization process is:

1. Replace the default brand elements, written content, and imagery.
2. Modify the pricing areas to reflect your coaching packages.
3. Substitute the Razorpay URL with the payment link connected to your account.
4. Rewrite the WhatsApp onboarding message for your workflow.
5. Check the intake modal fields and supplement enquiry actions.
6. Deploy the completed page to your chosen host.

When testing locally, open the page in a browser and confirm that pricing actions, payment links, and messaging controls lead to the intended destinations.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Can the pricing and contact information be replaced?**  
Yes. Edit the page content to substitute your own services, prices, and contact details.

**Where is the payment destination configured?**  
Find the Razorpay link in the page markup and change it to the payment destination associated with your account.

**How can I change the WhatsApp onboarding text?**  
Update the WhatsApp URL or its message parameter in the applicable HTML section.

**Why might the page appear differently on a phone?**  
The layout is designed to be responsive, but custom text, images, and spacing can affect the result. Test those changes on smaller screens before publishing.

**Where can I find deployment assistance?**  
Consult the documentation for your hosting provider and check that the page paths and external URLs are correct.
